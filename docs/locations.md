# Lost & found locations

## Rigby Hall 144

- **Name:** Rigby Hall
- **Office:** Rigby Hall 144
- **Latitude:** 43.817056
- **Longitude:** -111.7844505
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-5:00 PM
- **Hold period:** 60 days
- **Source:** BYU-Idaho Lost and Found page

The official BYU-Idaho Lost and Found Office is in Rigby Hall 144. Items held by
the office are retained for 60 days before the Lost and Found Sale.
